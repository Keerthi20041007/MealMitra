WHY MEALMITRA?

Every meal deserves a chance to make a difference.

Every day, perfectly good food is thrown away while many people struggle to find their next meal. MealMitra bridges this gap by connecting surplus food with organizations that can distribute it to communities in need.

ABOUT MEALMITRA

Food should be shared, not wasted.

MealMitra is a food donation and distribution platform designed to connect food donors with NGOs and volunteers.

Our goal is simple: make it easier for surplus food to reach people who need it while reducing unnecessary food waste.
